# Verified reference notes

Checked against primary publisher/author/model documentation on 11 November 2026. These are a starting reading list, not a completed literature review. Read the sources, select only claims actually used and renumber in first-citation order in the student's report. Do not reuse source performance numbers as ProductLens results.

1. K. Peffers, T. Tuunanen, M. A. Rothenberger, and S. Chatterjee, “A design science research methodology for information systems research,” Journal of Management Information Systems, vol. 24, no. 3, pp. 45–77, 2007, doi: 10.2753/MIS0742-1222240302. [Publisher](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.2753/MIS0742-1222240302); [author-hosted paper](https://scholar.cgu.edu/samir-chatterjee/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2013/07/jmis-article.pdf). Publisher identifies 2007; issue states Winter 2007–8 and copyright 2008. Use for DSR process, not a claim that this project's evaluation establishes usefulness.
2. N. Reimers and I. Gurevych, “Sentence-BERT: Sentence embeddings using Siamese BERT-networks,” in Proceedings of EMNLP-IJCNLP, 2019, pp. 3982–3992, doi: 10.18653/v1/D19-1410. [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1410/). Use for sentence embedding/cosine similarity background; the deployed MiniLM checkpoint is identified separately.
3. Sentence Transformers, “all-MiniLM-L6-v2,” Hugging Face, model card. [Online]. Available: https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2. Accessed: Nov. 11, 2026. Model maps text into 384-dimensional vectors; ProductLens pins revision 1110a243fdf4706b3f48f1d95db1a4f5529b4d41. Card describes default truncation beyond 256 word pieces; local app flags truncation.
4. S. McBride, “RICE: Simple prioritization for product managers,” Intercom, Jan. 5, 2018. [Online]. Available: https://www.intercom.com/blog/rice-simple-prioritization-for-product-managers/. Accessed: Nov. 11, 2026. Practitioner source for reach × impact × confidence / effort. Our customer/urgency/person-day adaptation differs and must be labelled RICE-style proxy.
5. Scikit-learn developers, “Clustering performance evaluation,” scikit-learn 1.9.1 documentation. [Online]. Available: https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/clustering.html#clustering-performance-evaluation. Accessed: Nov. 11, 2026. Metric implementation background for ARI/NMI and clustering definitions. It does not validate this synthetic catalog or ranking proxies.

For the literature review, compare what these sources support with what remains unresolved: domain-specific product feedback, multi-intent grouping, reliable effort estimates, strategic values, independent labels, and actual human decision utility. A broad literature-review claim needs additional verified sources; do not assert exhaustive coverage from five references.
